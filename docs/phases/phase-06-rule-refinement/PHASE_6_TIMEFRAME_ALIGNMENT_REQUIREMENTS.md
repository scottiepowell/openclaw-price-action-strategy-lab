# Phase 6 Timeframe Alignment Requirements

## Purpose

Define the timeframe alignment needed for a refined candidate.

## Requirements

- relevant higher-timeframe context is known
- lower-timeframe setup does not contradict higher-timeframe bias
- the candidate notes which timeframe drives the decision
- conflicts are treated as blockers, not noise

## Fail fast conditions

- conflicting higher-timeframe direction
- unclear timeframe priority
- setup depends on ignoring the higher timeframe
