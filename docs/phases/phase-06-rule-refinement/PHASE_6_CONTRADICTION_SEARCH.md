# Phase 6 Contradiction Search

## Purpose

Define the contradiction search needed before paper validation.

## Search goal

Find evidence that weakens, splits, or rejects the candidate.

## What to look for

- examples where the target fails
- examples where the setup appears valid but is later invalidated
- examples showing the candidate is really two behaviors
- examples where higher-timeframe context defeats the setup
- examples where no-trade filters should have blocked it

## Required outcome

- contradictions are documented
- contradictions are tied to evidence
- unresolved contradictions prevent promotion

## Fail fast conditions

- contradiction search was skipped
- contradiction notes are vague
- contradictions are only inferred after the fact
